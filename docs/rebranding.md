# Renamed from Moray

Jasper was called Moray before it was renamed. Records made before the rename keep the old name: the 2026-09-12
benchmark manifests in [`benchmarks/`](benchmarks) list the measured jars as `moray-app.jar` and `moray-terminal.jar`
with their original hashes, and the [macOS Local Network verification](mac-local-network-verification.md) quotes the
old bundle identifier `dev.moray.app`.
